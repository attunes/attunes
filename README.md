### Hi, I'm Joshua.

Full-stack developer based in Rotterdam. I take projects from idea to live: frontend, backend, hosting, deployment, and the small details that make a product feel finished. I pick the right tools for the job instead of forcing one stack onto every build.

Right now I'm building **ContextSnip**, a cross-platform desktop app that turns screen recordings into AI-friendly annotated screenshots and transcripts, and **SoWhatsThePlan**, a calmer way to plan dream trips. Alongside that, I do client work end-to-end.

### Currently

- Building client work and internal tooling end-to-end
- Studying Computer Science at Hogeschool Rotterdam
- Working through Azure certs: AZ-900 done, AZ-104 next
- Open to freelance work, especially useful tools, websites, and launch-ready systems
