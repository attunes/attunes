## Hi, I'm Joshua.

Full-stack developer based in Rotterdam. I take projects from idea to live — frontend, backend, hosting, all of it. I pick the right tools for the job rather than forcing one stack onto every problem.

Right now I'm building **ContextSnip**, a cross-platform desktop app that turns screen recordings into AI-friendly annotated screenshots and transcripts, and **SoWhatsThePlan**, the best way to plan out your dream trips. On the side I'm doing client work — building and shipping their stuff end-to-end.
